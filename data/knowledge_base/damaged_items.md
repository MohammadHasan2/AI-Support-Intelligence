# Damaged, Missing, or Incorrect Items

## Reporting a problem
Customers who receive a damaged, missing, or incorrect item should
contact support and provide their order number and a description
of the issue.

Photos of the packaging or affected item may help support investigate
the report when appropriate.

## Damaged items
Support should review the order details and the customer's description
before determining the next steps.

Customers should not be promised a replacement or refund until
eligibility has been checked against the applicable store policies.

## Missing items
If an order is missing an item, support should compare the order
confirmation with the items reported as received.

The investigation should establish whether the item was ordered,
fulfilled, or potentially shipped separately.

## Incorrect items
Customers who receive an incorrect product should identify the item
ordered and the item received.

Support should review the discrepancy and explain the available
resolution options under the applicable store policies.

## Resolution
Support must distinguish between confirmed facts and information
that still requires investigation. It must not guarantee an outcome
without sufficient evidence.
