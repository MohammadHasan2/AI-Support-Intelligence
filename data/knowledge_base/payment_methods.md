# Payment Methods and Billing Support

## Payment confirmation
Customers should check their order confirmation and account order history
to verify whether an order was successfully placed.

If a payment appears to have been processed but no order confirmation
was received, the customer should contact support with the order details
or transaction reference.

## Duplicate charges
If a customer believes they were charged more than once, support should
review the transaction references and order records before confirming
whether a duplicate charge occurred.

Customers should not assume that two pending transactions represent
two completed payments.

## Payment failures
If a payment fails, customers should verify their payment details and
contact their payment provider if the issue continues.

Support must not request a customer's password, full card number,
security code, or banking login credentials.

## Billing disputes
Customers who believe a charge is incorrect should contact support
with the transaction date, amount, and relevant order number.
Support should investigate the available records before giving a
definitive explanation.
